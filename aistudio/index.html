aiStudio

aiStudio is a personal tool that makes short explainer videos and saves the finished videos, with their captions, to its owner's own Google Drive.

It is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no accounts or sign-ups.

Google Drive access

aiStudio asks Google for one permission, drive.file: it can see and change only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read anything else in the Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.
