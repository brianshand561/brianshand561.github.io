aiStudio privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

aiStudio is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy covers the data it handles through Google APIs.

What it accesses

aiStudio requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, aiStudio can create folders and files in its owner's Google Drive and update the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

What it does with that access

It uploads videos and caption text files that its owner made with aiStudio into a folder it created in the owner's Drive, and replaces those files when a video is re-rendered. That is the only use.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. aiStudio keeps no other Google user data and runs no servers that receive it.

Sharing

aiStudio does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files aiStudio created stay in the Drive until deleted there.
